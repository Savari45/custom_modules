Odoo POS Barcode Screen

Reduce human efforts and make order placement faster!!

Odoo POS Barcode Screen module provides an enhanced barcode screen for the POS user to see more details about the scanned products. The POS user can directly scan the products and it will be listed on the POS store. It will reduce human efforts and customer's time thus, providing a user friendly experience.

Scan products and add them in the cart easily!!
Generally in POS, whenever a seller scans a product from a barcode reader, products are added in the cart which are unidentifiable due to the small size of cart.
With many customers already standing in queue, it's our responsibility to place their orders quickly. So, implementing Odoo POS Barcode Screen which allows you to display a different screen with a detailed view of the product whenever the product is scanned.
The module provides a better screen for scanning products using barcodes in POS. It reduces the manual effort of adding products in the cart one by one. Also, sellers will be able to see the detailed view of the scanned product along with the product image.
Barcodes are the perfect solution to all your problems. It enables you to enhance the inventory of your products, if you have a large product line. The bar-code technology lets you track all materials received instantly, stored, and shipped. Cashier can view all information about the product and its inventory quite quickly!

Provide Barcode Screen For Scanned Products In Odoo

  • Odoo POS barcode screen module provides an additional barcode screen for scanned products.
  • This barcode screen can be opened and closed anytime.

View The Detailed Description Of Products In POS

  • The cashier can view a detailed description of the product on the barcode screen.
  • A POS user can select any product from the list of scanned products and view the product's image and general information in the POS running session.
